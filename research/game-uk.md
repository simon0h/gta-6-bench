# GAME (UK) — pre-ordering Grand Theft Auto VI

Researched 2026-09-29 with the built-in browser (own tab, cookies rejected, nothing typed, no sign-in, no order) plus GAME's help centre. GAME is owned by Frasers Group and runs on the same web platform as Sports Direct, so several field names (Colour, Size) are platform boilerplate.

**Summary:** Pre-order is live. Two Standard-edition listings, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, both £69.99, both "code in box" (no disc), release 19 Nov 2026, pre-order bonus Vintage Vice City Pack. No Ultimate edition (it is digital-only and GAME does not sell digital GTA VI). Home delivery only (Click & Collect is excluded for pre-orders). Card is authorised at order and debited at dispatch, the day before launch. Guest checkout is offered after an email-first sign-in screen. PayPal is not accepted for pre-orders.

## Quirks

1. Search lives at `/searchresults?descriptionfilter=…`; `/search?q=` 404s.
2. Platform = "Colour" swatch (PS5 / XBS) plus a hidden "One Size" size select — Frasers/Sports Direct platform boilerplate.
3. One product code per platform (756802 PS5, 400064 Xbox); no edition selector; no Ultimate edition, no digital SKU.
4. Physical copy is a download code in a box — "A disc will not be included in the box."
5. Two-modal add-to-bag: Pre-order confirmation → PEGI-18 Age Verification ("I'm Old Enough").
6. Live "N days N hours left to pre-order" countdown.
7. Whole order held until every line is available; pre-orders ship Express the day before launch.
8. Click & Collect exists but is switched off for pre-order items — GTA VI is home-delivery only.
9. PayPal is accepted in general but not for pre-orders.
10. Authorise at order, debit at dispatch; no deposit; pre-orders cannot be amended.
11. Email-first auth on `auth.game.co.uk` with invisible reCAPTCHA; guest option appears after the email step.
12. Bag kept 30 days, but "Items in bag aren't reserved!".
13. Akamai Bot Manager: automated add-to-bag stalled on "Adding..." — expect bot friction.
14. In-store pre-orders/deposits ended 1 Aug 2024; GAME Reward/Elite ended 31 Jul 2024; Frasers Plus (BNPL, 29.9% APR rep.) is the only "membership"-style upsell.
15. Cookie banner (Reject all / Allow all) and a possible geo prompt ("Go to our website" / "Stay on the website") on first load.
16. Quantity default 1, max 99 per line.
