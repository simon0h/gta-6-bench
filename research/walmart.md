#### Why there is no guest checkout (verified 2026-09-29)
- The identity app's login bundle contains an `Or` divider and a **`Continue as guest`** button (i18n key `continueAsGuestBtnLabel`, analytics name `continueAsGuest`), rendered only when the URL carries `showGXO=true` (internally `showGuestCTA` → `sgc`).
- The walmart.com cart page's embedded feature config says `enableGuestCheckout: true` but **`hideGuestXOCTA: true`**, `enableFullPageSignin: true`, `enableSignInFromCart: true`. The cart's routing code only appends `&showGXO=true` when `hideGuestXOCTA` is false, so shoppers get the URL above (with `hidePC=true&tp=CXO`) and never see the guest button.
- Forcing `&showGXO=true` onto the identity URL does render `Or` / `Continue as guest` under the `Continue` button, but clicking it only returns to `https://www.walmart.com/?gxo=true`. Loading `https://www.walmart.com/cart?gxo=true` auto-forwards to the real checkout URL `https://www.walmart.com/checkout/review-order?cartId=<uuid>`, and the checkout's auth middleware immediately bounces to `identity.walmart.com/account/login?tp=AuthMiddlewareCsr&…&state=%2Fcheckout%2Freview-order%3FcartId%3D<uuid>` — the same sign-in screen, still without a guest button.
- After an unrecognized identifier the identity app routes to `/account/sign-up` (`Create your Walmart account`); that page's bundle has no guest option.
- Leftover guest plumbing exists (the thank-you page has a `Just one more thing...` / `Set your password - track your order easily and check out even faster next time` module for guest orders), so Walmart could re-enable it, but for the benchmark: **an account with a verified phone number is required.**

## Quirks
1. `Preorder` is the CTA label everywhere (search tile, PDP, related carousels); clicking it hard-redirects to a full-page `Added to cart!` interstitial (`/pac?…`) instead of a modal — the shopper must click `View cart (1)`.
2. Listing titles embed the logistics: `(Code in Box, Delivers 11/12/26, Playable 11/19/26)`; Walmart's `Release date Nov 12` badge is the ship/preload date, not the Nov 19 play date.
3. Separate listings per platform, no variant selector; no Ultimate Edition on walmart.com.
4. **No guest checkout for US shoppers.** `Continue to checkout` goes straight to `identity.walmart.com` with one `Phone number or email (required)` field. The guest button exists in the identity app but is suppressed by the cart flag `hideGuestXOCTA: true`, and the checkout's auth middleware bounces unauthenticated `/checkout/review-order` requests back to sign-in. New shoppers go through `Create your Walmart account` with mandatory SMS phone verification.
5. Sign-in is one-time-code friendly: recognized accounts may be offered `Text me a verification code` / `Email me a verification code` / `Enter your password`, new devices get a `Verify it's you` code step, and `Sign in with passkey` is available.
6. Location/store context (`Pickup or delivery? Omaha, 68116 • …`) drives the PDP fulfillment tiles (`Shipping / Pickup / Delivery`) and the cart's ZIP button; for this preorder only `Shipping — Free — Arrives Nov 12` is live (`Pickup: Check nearby`, `Delivery: Not available`). Checkout has no fulfillment switcher, just the `Shipping — Arrives by …` card.
7.
